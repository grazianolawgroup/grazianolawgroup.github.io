Attorney Profile

Philip W. Graziano, Esq.

Philip W. Graziano is the founder and principal attorney of Graziano Law Group, PLLC, based in Pembroke Pines, Florida. He represents individuals and families across South Florida in Estate Planning, Family Law, Real Estate, and Traffic Ticket matters.

Mr. Graziano built his practice around a simple idea: clients deserve direct access to the attorney handling their case, clear explanations of their options, and honest advice at every step — not a revolving door of staff and unreturned calls.

He works closely with each client to understand their goals, whether that means drafting an estate plan that protects a family's future, guiding someone through a difficult family law matter, closing a real estate transaction smoothly, or defending against a traffic citation that could affect a driving record or insurance rates.
