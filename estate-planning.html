What's Included

A well-drafted estate plan protects your family, your assets, and your wishes — and spares your loved ones unnecessary stress and expense down the road. Graziano Law Group works with clients throughout Broward County to build estate plans tailored to their circumstances, whether simple or complex.

  • Last wills and testaments
  • Revocable and irrevocable trusts
  • Durable powers of attorney
  • Healthcare surrogate designations and living wills
  • Beneficiary planning and asset protection strategies
  • Probate and estate administration guidance
