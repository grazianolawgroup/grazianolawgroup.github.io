Estate Planning

A well-drafted estate plan protects your family, your assets, and your wishes — and spares your loved ones unnecessary stress and expense down the road. Graziano Law Group works with clients to build estate plans tailored to their circumstances, whether simple or complex.

  • Last wills and testaments
  • Revocable and irrevocable trusts
  • Durable powers of attorney
  • Healthcare surrogate designations and living wills
  • Beneficiary planning and asset protection strategies
  • Probate and estate administration guidance
🎥 Available Virtually

Family Law

Family law matters are personal and often emotional. Attorney Graziano provides steady, practical guidance to help clients navigate these situations with clarity and protect what matters most.

  • Divorce and marital settlement agreements
  • Child custody, time-sharing, and parenting plans
  • Child support and alimony
  • Paternity actions
  • Modifications and enforcement of existing orders
  • Prenuptial and postnuptial agreements
🎥 Available Virtually

Traffic Tickets

A traffic citation can mean more than a fine — it can raise your insurance rates, add points to your license, or put your driving privileges at risk. The firm represents clients throughout South Florida in contesting traffic tickets and minimizing the consequences.

  • Moving violations and speeding tickets
  • Red light and stop sign citations
  • Careless or reckless driving charges
  • License point mitigation
  • Civil traffic infraction hearings
🎥 Available Virtually

Real Estate

Whether you're buying, selling, or resolving a dispute, real estate transactions involve significant sums and binding contracts. Graziano Law Group provides attentive guidance to help clients close with confidence.

  • Residential and commercial purchase and sale contracts
  • Contract review and negotiation
  • Closings and title issues
  • Landlord-tenant matters
  • Deed preparation and transfers
