Privacy Policy

How Graziano Law Group, PLLC collects, uses, and protects information submitted through this website.

Graziano Law Group, PLLC ("the firm," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through grazianolawgroup.com (the "site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies only to this website and does not extend to any third-party sites we may link to.

Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

Information you provide directly. When you submit the contact form on our Contact page, we collect the information you enter: your name, email address, phone number (optional), matter type, meeting preference, and message. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and, if you engage the firm, to provide legal services.

Automatically collected information. Like most websites, we automatically receive certain technical information when you visit the site, such as your browser type, device type, approximate location, referring page, and pages viewed. This is collected through Google Analytics, described below.

Third-Party Services We Use

We rely on a small number of third-party services to operate the site. Each processes limited data on our behalf:

Contact form delivery (FormSubmit). Messages submitted through our contact form are delivered to us using FormSubmit, a third-party form-processing service. The information you enter is transmitted to FormSubmit and forwarded to our email inbox; it is not used by FormSubmit for advertising.
Website analytics (Google Analytics). We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use the site — for example, which pages are viewed and how visitors arrived at the site. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect this information. We do not use Google Analytics to identify individual visitors.
Fonts (Google Fonts). Our site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser connects directly to Google's servers to retrieve these fonts, which may transmit your IP address to Google.
Office map (Google Maps). Our Contact page embeds a Google Maps view of our office location. Interacting with the embedded map is subject to Google's own privacy policy.

Each of these providers has its own privacy practices, which we encourage you to review: FormSubmit's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy (covering Analytics, Fonts, and Maps).

Cookies

Google Analytics places a small number of cookies on your device to distinguish visitors and sessions. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics using Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Blocking cookies will not affect your ability to browse the site or submit the contact form.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to respond to consultation requests, communicate with prospective and current clients, operate and improve the site, and comply with our legal and professional obligations. We do not use information submitted through the contact form for marketing to third parties.

We Do Not Sell Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for their own marketing purposes.

Data Retention & Security

We retain information submitted through the contact form for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and, where an attorney-client relationship is formed, in accordance with our professional recordkeeping obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect information transmitted through the site, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may decline to submit information through our contact form and instead reach us by phone or email using the contact details on this page. If you would like us to delete information you previously submitted, contact us using the information below and we will address your request.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The "Effective" date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.
