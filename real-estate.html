Common Questions

Real Estate FAQ

Do I need an attorney to close on a home in Florida?

Florida doesn't require an attorney for every closing, but having one review your contract and represent your interests at closing can help catch issues before they become expensive problems, particularly for title defects or contract terms.

What is title insurance and do I need it?

Title insurance protects against losses from defects in the property's title that weren't discovered during the title search, such as liens, fraud, or errors in public records. Lenders typically require it, and owner's title insurance is worth considering even when not required.

What should I do before signing a purchase contract?

Have the contract reviewed before signing. Key terms — financing contingencies, inspection periods, closing timelines, and what happens if either party doesn't perform — are easier to negotiate before signatures than after.

Can you help with landlord-tenant disputes?

Yes. The firm assists both landlords and tenants with lease disputes, eviction-related matters, and related real estate issues in Broward County.
