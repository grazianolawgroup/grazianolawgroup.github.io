Service Areas

Graziano Law Group, PLLC meets clients in person throughout Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach Counties from our Pembroke Pines office — and handles matters virtually for clients anywhere in Florida.

🎥 In Person

Broward County

Home to our Pembroke Pines office, and the county we serve most often in person. Clients regularly come to us from Pembroke Pines, Miramar, Cooper City, Davie, Weston, Hollywood, Plantation, Sunrise, Fort Lauderdale, Pompano Beach, and Coral Springs.

🎥 In Person

Miami-Dade County

We regularly meet with clients from Miami, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, Doral, Hialeah, Aventura, Kendall, and Homestead — in our office or, where appropriate, by video consultation for convenience.

🎥 In Person

Palm Beach County

Clients throughout West Palm Beach, Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Boynton Beach, Wellington, and Jupiter can meet with Attorney Graziano in person or virtually, depending on what's most convenient.

🎥 Available Virtually

Rest of Florida

Outside of South Florida, every practice area is available virtually — video consultations, secure e-signing, and phone or email follow-up — so clients anywhere in the state can work with our office without needing to travel.
