Common Questions

Traffic Ticket FAQ

Should I just pay the ticket?

Paying a ticket is treated as an admission of guilt and typically adds points to your license and can raise insurance premiums. Depending on the circumstances, contesting the citation or pursuing options like traffic school may better protect your record.

What happens if I ignore a traffic ticket in Florida?

Ignoring a citation can lead to additional fines, a license suspension, or a warrant in some cases. It's important to respond by the date on the citation, even if you plan to contest it.

Will fighting a ticket keep it off my record?

Outcomes vary by case, citation type, and jurisdiction. Contesting a ticket creates the opportunity to avoid points or a conviction, but results depend on the specific facts and evidence involved.

Do I have to appear in court for a traffic ticket?

It depends on the type of citation and whether you're represented. In many civil traffic infraction cases, an attorney can appear on your behalf, which is one of the practical benefits of hiring counsel.
